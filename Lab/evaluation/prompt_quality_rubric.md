# Prompt Quality Rubric

## Scoring Scale

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Poor – Major deficiencies; does not meet expectations. |
| 2 | Fair – Significant improvements required. |
| 3 | Good – Meets basic expectations with minor gaps. |
| 4 | Very Good – Strong performance with minimal issues. |
| 5 | Excellent – Fully meets or exceeds expectations. |

---

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|-----------|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Information is incorrect or misleading. | Multiple inaccuracies present. | Mostly accurate with minor errors. | Accurate with very few issues. | Completely accurate and reliable. |
| Clarity | Difficult to understand. | Somewhat unclear and disorganized. | Generally understandable. | Clear and well-structured. | Exceptionally clear and concise. |
| Completeness | Major requirements missing. | Several important gaps. | Most requirements covered. | Nearly all requirements covered. | Fully comprehensive and complete. |
| Consistency | Contradictory or inconsistent. | Frequent inconsistencies. | Mostly consistent. | Consistent throughout. | Fully consistent in content and format. |
| Relevance | Not aligned with objectives. | Limited relevance. | Generally relevant. | Highly relevant. | Directly aligned with business needs and objectives. |
| Professionalism | Unprofessional tone and style. | Some professionalism issues. | Acceptable professional tone. | Professional and polished. | Executive-level professionalism throughout. |
| Business Value | Provides little or no value. | Limited actionable value. | Some useful insights. | Strong actionable recommendations. | High-impact business value with clear outcomes. |
