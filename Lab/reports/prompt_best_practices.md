# Prompt Best Practices for Enterprise AI Solutions

## Overview

This document summarizes key best practices identified during prompt engineering activities and provides recommendations for creating effective enterprise-grade prompts.

---

## 1. Define a Clear Role

- Assign a specific role to the AI model.
- Clearly establish expertise and responsibilities.
- Use role definitions aligned with business objectives.
- Ensure the role reflects the expected perspective and decision-making authority.

**Example:**
"You are a Senior Financial Analyst responsible for evaluating quarterly business performance."

---

## 2. Provide Business Context

- Include relevant organizational background.
- Explain the business problem being addressed.
- Specify industry, stakeholders, and operational environment.
- Supply sufficient context to improve response quality.

---

## 3. State a Clear Objective

- Define the desired outcome.
- Use measurable and actionable goals.
- Focus on a single primary objective whenever possible.
- Ensure success criteria can be evaluated.

---

## 4. Give Detailed Instructions

- Break complex tasks into logical steps.
- Specify required analysis or reasoning activities.
- Describe expected deliverables.
- Prioritize tasks where appropriate.

---

## 5. Define Constraints Explicitly

- State compliance requirements.
- Identify prohibited actions or assumptions.
- Define scope limitations.
- Specify formatting and content restrictions.

---

## 6. Specify Output Format

- Provide a consistent response structure.
- Use headings, bullet points, and sections.
- Define required tables, summaries, or recommendations.
- Standardize outputs for enterprise consumption.

---

## 7. Use Grounding Information

- Reference trusted business documents.
- Use approved knowledge sources.
- Reduce hallucinations through evidence-based prompting.
- Encourage source-based reasoning where applicable.

---

## 8. Maintain Professional Language

- Use business-appropriate terminology.
- Avoid ambiguity and informal expressions.
- Ensure communication is concise and actionable.
- Align language with organizational standards.

---

## 9. Test and Refine Prompts

- Conduct multiple validation cycles.
- Measure accuracy and consistency.
- Record prompt versions.
- Continuously improve based on feedback.

---

## 10. Establish Evaluation Criteria

Evaluate prompts using:

- Accuracy
- Clarity
- Completeness
- Consistency
- Relevance
- Professionalism
- Business Value

---

## Enterprise Prompt Writing Recommendations

1. Start with a clearly defined role.
2. Include sufficient business context.
3. Specify measurable objectives.
4. Provide step-by-step instructions.
5. Define constraints and compliance requirements.
6. Standardize output formats.
7. Use reliable grounding information.
8. Evaluate prompts against formal quality metrics.
9. Maintain version history and documentation.
10. Continuously optimize prompts using testing results.

---

## Conclusion

Well-structured prompts improve response quality, consistency, governance, and business value. Following these best practices enables organizations to build reliable, scalable, and high-quality AI-powered solutions for enterprise use cases.
