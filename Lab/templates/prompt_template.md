# Prompt Template

## Role
Define the role the AI should assume.

**Example:**
You are a Senior Business Analyst with expertise in process optimization and stakeholder management.

---

## Business Context
Provide relevant background information, organizational context, industry details, and any important assumptions.

**Example:**
The organization is implementing a new customer onboarding process to reduce manual effort and improve customer satisfaction.

---

## Objective
Clearly state the goal or expected outcome.

**Example:**
Analyze the current onboarding workflow and recommend improvements to reduce onboarding time by 30%.

---

## Instructions
Provide detailed execution steps and guidance.

**Example:**
1. Review the provided process documentation.
2. Identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies.
3. Recommend process improvements.
4. Highlight potential risks and dependencies.
5. Provide actionable recommendations.

---

## Constraints
Specify limitations, exclusions, or compliance requirements.

**Example:**
- Do not make assumptions beyond the provided information.
- Follow company compliance guidelines.
- Use professional business language.
- Limit recommendations to realistic, implementable solutions.

---

## Output Format
Define the expected structure and formatting of the response.

**Example:**

### Executive Summary
Brief overview of findings.

### Current State Analysis
Detailed assessment of existing processes.

### Recommendations
- Recommendation 1
- Recommendation 2
- Recommendation 3

### Risks and Considerations
List potential risks and mitigation strategies.

### Next Steps
Action plan with priorities.

---

## Success Criteria
Describe how success will be measured.

**Example:**
- Recommendations are actionable and realistic.
- Analysis is supported by provided information.
- Risks and dependencies are clearly identified.
- Output follows the specified format.
- Stakeholders can use the recommendations for decision-making.

---
